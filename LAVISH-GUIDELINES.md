## 01. BRAND IDENTITY

Lavish is a streetwear brand rooted in hip-hop culture, underground communities and contemporary high fashion.

Its visual identity combines raw street culture with premium fashion aesthetics.

The brand draws inspiration from:

* Hip-hop culture and music
* Basketball and football
* Underground fashion
* Graffiti and street art
* Rooftops, parking garages and underground locations
* U-Bahn stations, streets and everyday environments
* Dancers, musicians and authentic personalities

The goal is to create a recognizable visual identity, not generic social media content.

## 02. VISUAL DIRECTION

Core aesthetic:

* Raw
* Dark
* Underground
* Cinematic
* Authentic
* Editorial
* Imperfect but intentional
* Premium without looking commercial

Avoid making footage look overly clean, corporate, polished or artificial.

The footage should feel like a combination of underground hip-hop documentation and high-fashion editorial filmmaking.

## 03. COLOR GRADING

* Slightly desaturated colors.
* Deep blacks while retaining image details.
* Natural skin tones.
* Subtle cool undertones where appropriate.
* Preserve natural lighting and environmental colors.
* Use yellow as a recognizable visual accent.
* Apply subtle film grain when appropriate.

Never apply excessive filters or identical grading to every scene.

## 04. EDITING LANGUAGE

Preferred editing techniques:

* Hard cuts
* Intentional jump cuts
* Rhythmic cuts
* Occasional freeze frames
* Unexpected framing
* Documentary-style sequences
* Mixed pacing
* Short bursts of fast editing followed by longer shots

Avoid:

* Generic CapCut transitions
* Excessive zoom effects
* Unnecessary motion graphics
* Overused glitch effects
* Cutting on every single beat
* Repetitive transitions

The editing should feel spontaneous but remain carefully constructed.

## 05. TEXT AND TYPOGRAPHY

* Use the provided Lavish font.
* Yellow is the primary subtitle accent.
* Keep subtitles visually consistent.
* Use editorial typography sparingly.
* Integrate small text elements when they add context.
* Avoid generic influencer captions and emoji-based graphics.

Typography should feel inspired by underground magazines, music editorials and streetwear campaigns.

## 06. TEXTURE AND MEDIA

Use different visual textures where appropriate:

* 35mm film grain
* VHS and MiniDV footage
* Flash photography
* Documentary footage
* Analog overlays
* Photocopy and magazine textures

These elements should be used selectively. Do not overload the entire video with effects.

## 07. SOUND DESIGN

Music is important, but environmental audio is equally valuable.

Preserve authentic ambient sounds and use subtle sound design to enhance the atmosphere.

Avoid excessive whooshes, artificial impacts and generic commercial sound effects.

## 08. PRODUCT PRESENTATION

Lavish clothing should feel like part of the environment, not like a traditional product advertisement.

Highlight:

* Fabric texture
* Silhouette
* Fit
* Movement
* Distressing and construction details
* Individual styling

Do not constantly force logos into the viewer's attention.

## 09. CREATIVE RULE

Never make a video look like a generic fashion influencer Reel.

Never sacrifice the brand identity just to follow a social media trend.

Every edit should feel like it belongs to the Lavish universe.

The final result should communicate:

STREET CULTURE. CHARACTER. IDENTITY. ATTITUDE.

When in doubt, choose authenticity over perfection and atmosphere over unnecessary effects.
